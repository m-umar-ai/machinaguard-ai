# Centrifugal Pump Cavitation and Process Faults
## Cavitation symptoms
Cavitation sounds like gravel in the pump and produces broadband high-frequency vibration, falling discharge pressure and unstable flow. Causes include low suction head, blocked strainers, high fluid temperature and excessive flow.
## Process faults
A drop in pressure or production rate without a matching vibration or temperature rise suggests a process or hydraulic issue such as a blocked line, valve position or upstream supply, not a bearing fault.
## Corrective action
Check suction conditions and strainers, confirm valve positions and compare operating point with the pump curve. Do not run a pump dry or against a closed discharge.
