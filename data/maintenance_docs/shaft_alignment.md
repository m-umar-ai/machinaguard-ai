# Shaft Alignment Procedure
## Symptoms of misalignment
Misalignment typically produces vibration at one and two times running speed, often with a strong axial component, elevated coupling temperature and higher energy draw. Parallel and angular misalignment frequently occur together.
## Alignment method
Isolate and lock out the machine. Check for soft foot first, then align using a laser alignment tool or dial indicators, correcting vertical before horizontal. Compare results against the coupling manufacturer's tolerance for the operating speed.
## After alignment
Re-torque foundation bolts, run the machine and record vibration in all three directions. Thermal growth between cold and running states may need an intentional offset on large machines.
