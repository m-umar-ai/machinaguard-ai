# Belt Drive and Coupling Inspection
## Belt drives
Worn, glazed or mismatched belts slip and create heat and speed loss; over-tensioned belts overload bearings. Replace belts as a matched set and check sheave wear and alignment.
## Couplings
Inspect flexible couplings for cracks, hardened elements and fretting. A failing coupling raises vibration at one and two times running speed and may produce noise on start-up.
## Records
Record belt tension and coupling element condition at each service so degradation can be tracked.
