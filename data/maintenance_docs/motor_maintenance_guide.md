# Motor Maintenance Guide
## Electrical overload indicators
Rising energy consumption with falling rpm indicates increased mechanical load or winding problems. Check phase current balance, insulation resistance and supply voltage.
## Cooling and lubrication
High motor temperature with normal vibration suggests blocked ventilation, fan failure or insufficient lubrication. Clean cooling passages and verify grease quantity and type.
## Mechanical coupling
Increased energy draw together with vibration can indicate shaft misalignment or a seizing bearing. Inspect coupling alignment with a dial indicator or laser tool.
