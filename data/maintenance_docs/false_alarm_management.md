# Alarm Management and False Alarm Reduction
## Why false alarms matter
Frequent false alarms train operators to ignore alerts. Every alarm should be actionable and carry evidence the operator can check.
## Reducing false alarms
Require agreement between independent signals before escalating, use redundant sensors where failure is costly, apply trend and persistence rules, and show the confidence of each alert.
## Review
Review alarms regularly, tag each as true or false positive and tune thresholds from the record.
