# Root Cause Analysis After Failure
## Preserve evidence
After a failure, keep the failed parts, photograph the damage, and record operating conditions, alarms and recent maintenance before cleaning or discarding anything.
## Method
Use a structured method such as five whys or fault-tree analysis. Separate the failure mode from the cause: a spalled bearing is a failure mode; contamination, misalignment or lubrication loss may be the cause.
## Prevent recurrence
Assign corrective actions, update procedures and feed the finding back into alarm rules and diagnosis logic.
