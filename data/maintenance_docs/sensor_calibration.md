# Vibration Sensor Verification and Calibration
## Detecting a faulty sensor
A faulty accelerometer often reads high or erratic while redundant sensors, temperature, speed and power draw remain normal. Spikes, flat-lining, noise that is not repeatable at running speed, and large disagreement between redundant channels indicate a measurement problem.
## Verification procedure
Compare the suspect channel with a redundant sensor and a calibrated handheld meter at the same location. Inspect the mounting stud or magnet, the cable, connectors and shielding, and check the supply voltage and bias level of the sensor.
## Corrective action
Re-mount or replace the sensor and cable, calibrate against a reference shaker if available, and record the date. Avoid mechanical maintenance until the measurement has been confirmed by a second independent method.
