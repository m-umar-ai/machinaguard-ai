# Critical Spares and Replacement Policy
## Critical spares
Hold spare bearings, seals, couplings and a spare sensor for machines whose failure stops production. Lead time matters more than price for imported parts.
## Replace as a set
Replace bearings as a matched set with new seals, and re-grease to specification. Never reuse a removed bearing.
## Authorisation
Parts replacement based on condition data should be authorised by a qualified technician after physical inspection confirms the condition, because condition data alone gives a hypothesis, not proof.
