# Rotor Imbalance and Balancing
## Imbalance signature
Imbalance produces a dominant peak at one times running speed, mostly in the radial direction, with stable phase. Amplitude rises roughly with the square of speed. Temperature and power usually stay normal, which separates imbalance from bearing wear.
## Common causes
Material build-up on fan blades or impellers, lost balance weights, eroded or corroded blades, and thermal bowing. In dusty environments such as cement and textile plants, fan fouling is the most frequent cause.
## Corrective action
Clean the rotor first and re-measure. If vibration remains, perform single-plane or two-plane field balancing with a portable balancer and record before and after values.
