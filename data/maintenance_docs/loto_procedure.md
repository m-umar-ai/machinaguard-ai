# Lockout-Tagout Energy Isolation Procedure
## Purpose
Lockout-tagout ensures that a machine cannot start or release stored energy while people work on it. It applies to electrical, mechanical, hydraulic, pneumatic and gravitational energy sources.
## Steps
Notify affected staff, shut the machine down normally, isolate every energy source, apply personal locks and tags, release or block stored energy, then verify zero energy by attempting a start from the local control. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it.
## Return to service
Remove tools, replace guards, confirm everyone is clear, remove locks and tags, notify staff and restart under observation. Record the work in the maintenance log.
