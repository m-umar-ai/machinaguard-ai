# Voltage Fluctuation and Power Quality for Motors
## Effects of poor supply
Voltage sags, over-voltage, phase unbalance and frequent supply interruptions raise motor current and winding temperature and can reduce speed under load. In networks with unstable grid supply and generator changeover, repeated restarts add thermal and mechanical stress.
## Diagnosing a supply problem
A supply fault typically shows as energy and speed changes with little vibration increase, and it correlates with grid events or other machines on the same feeder. Measure all three phase voltages and currents; unbalance above a few percent needs correction.
## Protective measures
Use phase-failure and under/over-voltage relays, soft starters or drives on large motors, and delay restart after supply return so residual voltage does not cause a damaging out-of-phase restart. Consider power-quality logging on critical feeders.
