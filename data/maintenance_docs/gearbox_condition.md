# Gearbox Condition Monitoring
## Gear mesh signatures
Healthy gearboxes show gear-mesh frequency with low sidebands. Tooth wear, pitting or a cracked tooth increases sidebands spaced at shaft speed around the mesh frequency.
## Oil analysis
Regular oil sampling shows viscosity change, water contamination and wear particles. A rising count of ferrous particles is an early warning that precedes vibration changes.
## Response
Plan an inspection when sidebands or particle counts trend upward. Heavy industrial gearboxes in rolling mills and sugar mills justify combined vibration and oil monitoring.
