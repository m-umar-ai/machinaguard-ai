# Bearing Maintenance Guide
## Symptoms of bearing degradation
Rising broadband vibration is the earliest sign of rolling-element bearing wear. Temperature at the bearing housing follows as friction increases, and motor current or energy draw rises slightly. Rotational speed usually remains stable until late-stage failure. A combined rise in vibration, temperature and energy suggests advancing wear or lubrication breakdown.
## Inspection procedure
Isolate the machine under lockout-tagout. Remove the guard, inspect the bearing housing for discolouration, grease leakage and play. Measure radial clearance and compare with the manufacturer tolerance. Record findings before any component is replaced.
## Replacement criteria
Replace the bearing if radial clearance exceeds tolerance, if raceway pitting or spalling is visible, or if lubricant contains metallic particles. Always replace bearings as a matched set with new seals and re-grease to specification.
