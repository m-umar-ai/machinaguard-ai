# Mechanical Looseness and Soft Foot
## Looseness signature
Looseness produces multiple harmonics of running speed, sometimes half-order components, and erratic amplitude that changes with load. Directional vibration that is much higher in the vertical direction suggests a loose base or cracked frame.
## Soft foot
Soft foot occurs when a machine foot does not sit flat, distorting the frame when bolts are tightened. It causes vibration and bearing misalignment. Check by loosening each foot bolt in turn while measuring displacement with a dial indicator.
## Corrective action
Tighten bolts to specification, shim feet, re-grout or repair cracked bases, and repeat the measurement. Mark bolts after tightening so movement is visible.
