# Infrared Thermography Inspection
## What thermography finds
Infrared cameras reveal hot bearing housings, loose or oxidised electrical connections, overloaded phases and blocked cooling. Compare similar components under similar load; a difference between identical bearings is more informative than a single absolute temperature.
## Inspection practice
Scan under normal load, set the correct emissivity, avoid reflective surfaces and record images with the machine tag. Establish baseline images for critical machines so later scans can be compared.
## Interpreting results
A hot bearing with normal vibration points to lubrication or fit problems. A hot connection with uneven phase currents points to electrical faults. Always follow safety distance rules for energised equipment.
