# Vibration Severity Zones (ISO 10816 / 20816 Concepts)
## Severity zones
General machine vibration standards classify overall vibration velocity (mm/s RMS) into four zones: A (newly commissioned machines), B (acceptable for unrestricted long-term operation), C (unsatisfactory for long-term operation, plan corrective action) and D (vibration severe enough to cause damage). Exact boundaries depend on machine class, power rating and whether the foundation is rigid or flexible, so always take limits from the standard or the manufacturer for the specific machine.
## Using zones with baselines
A fixed zone limit is a blunt tool. Record a healthy baseline for each machine and alarm on sustained change from that baseline as well as on absolute zone limits. A machine that doubles its own baseline vibration while still inside zone B deserves investigation.
## Trend over snapshot
A single reading proves little. Look at the rate of change: a steady rise over days is wear, a sudden step is often a loose part, a damaged component or a sensor problem. Correlate vibration with temperature and power draw before escalating.
