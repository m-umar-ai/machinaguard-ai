# Large Fans and Mills in Cement and Steel Plants
## Fans
Induced-draft and cooling fans suffer blade fouling, erosion and imbalance in dusty service. Vibration rising at one times running speed after process changes usually points to build-up, not bearing wear.
## Mills and gearboxes
Mill drives and gearboxes carry very high loads. Monitor bearing temperature, oil condition and vibration together, and plan inspection during scheduled kiln or mill stops.
## Safety
Hot, dusty and confined environments require full isolation, permit to work, and gas or dust checks before entry.
