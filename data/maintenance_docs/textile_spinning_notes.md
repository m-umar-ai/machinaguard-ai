# Textile Mill Equipment Notes
## Spindles and ring frames
Ring frames and spinning machines run many spindles at high speed continuously. Spindle bearing wear shows as rising vibration and noise, uneven yarn quality and higher energy use. Fibre dust and lint accumulate in motors and bearings.
## Looms and drives
Weaving looms create cyclic shock loads; loose foundations and worn bearings are common. Humidification systems raise moisture, so protect electrical enclosures and check for corrosion.
## Practical notes
Clean lint from motor cooling paths, schedule inspections at planned stops, and track energy per unit of production because a rising ratio often reveals mechanical drag early.
