# Motor Overload and Current Analysis
## Overload symptoms
Overloaded motors draw higher current, run hotter and may lose slip speed. An energy increase together with falling RPM suggests mechanical overload or a supply issue rather than a bearing problem.
## Investigation steps
Compare measured current with nameplate full-load current. Check driven-equipment load, blocked discharge or jammed material, belt slip and coupling condition. Inspect cooling airflow and clean fan covers.
## When to stop
A sustained current above nameplate with rising winding temperature requires a controlled shutdown. Do not repeatedly reset a tripped overload relay without finding the cause.
