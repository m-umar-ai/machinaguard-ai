# Industrial Safety Procedure
## Lockout-tagout
No maintenance action may begin until the machine is isolated, de-energised and locked out by the authorised technician. Verify zero energy before touching any rotating part.
## Human authorisation
Automated systems must not initiate shutdown, repair or part replacement. A qualified supervisor must approve every maintenance action. Decision support systems provide recommendations only.
## Personal protective equipment
Wear safety glasses, gloves and hearing protection. Keep guards in place while the machine is running.
