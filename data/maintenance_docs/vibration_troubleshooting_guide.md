# Vibration Troubleshooting Guide
## Verify the measurement first
Before escalating a vibration alarm, verify the sensor. Compare against a redundant accelerometer or a handheld vibration meter. If one sensor reads high while a redundant sensor and temperature, speed and energy are normal, suspect sensor drift, loose mounting or cable noise rather than a mechanical fault.
## Common mechanical causes
Imbalance produces vibration at 1x running speed. Misalignment produces 2x harmonics and axial vibration. Bearing defects produce high-frequency content and are usually accompanied by a temperature rise. Looseness produces multiple harmonics and erratic amplitude.
## Alarm thresholds
Treat sustained vibration well above the healthy baseline as a warning. A rapid rise combined with temperature and energy increases is a critical condition requiring planned inspection at the earliest safe opportunity.
