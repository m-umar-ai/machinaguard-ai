# Bearing Defect Frequencies and Spectrum Analysis
## Characteristic frequencies
Rolling-element bearings produce defect frequencies determined by geometry and shaft speed: outer race (BPFO), inner race (BPFI), rolling element (BSF) and cage (FTF). An outer-race defect appears as a peak at BPFO and its harmonics; an inner-race defect appears at BPFI, often with sidebands at shaft speed.
## Early versus late stage
Early bearing damage excites high-frequency resonances and is best seen in envelope (demodulated) spectra. As damage grows, defect-frequency peaks and harmonics rise, then the noise floor lifts across the spectrum, and finally broadband vibration and housing temperature climb quickly.
## Limits of overall vibration
Overall RMS vibration is insensitive to early bearing damage. If the only data available is overall level, treat a rising trend together with temperature and power increase as a reason to collect a proper vibration spectrum rather than as a confirmed bearing fault.
