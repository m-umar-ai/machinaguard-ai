# Operating Rotating Equipment in Hot, Dusty Climates
## Heat
High ambient temperature reduces motor cooling margin and shortens grease life. Keep ventilation paths clear and consider more frequent lubrication checks in summer.
## Dust and humidity
Dust contaminates lubricants and blocks cooling fins; humidity and monsoon conditions cause corrosion and insulation degradation. Use sealed bearings and clean enclosures regularly.
## Baselines by season
Normal temperature varies with season, so interpret temperature against a baseline that accounts for ambient conditions.
