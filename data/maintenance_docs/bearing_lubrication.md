# Bearing Lubrication Practice
## Correct quantity and type
Both under-lubrication and over-lubrication shorten bearing life. Over-greasing raises churning friction and temperature; too little grease allows metal contact. Use the grease type and quantity specified by the bearing or machine manufacturer, and do not mix incompatible grease thickeners.
## Signs of lubrication breakdown
Rising housing temperature with only a modest vibration rise points toward lubrication problems rather than mechanical damage. Discoloured, hardened or leaking grease, and a burnt smell, confirm it. Contamination by dust or water accelerates failure in dusty plants.
## Regreasing discipline
Regrease at defined intervals using a clean grease gun, wipe the nipple first, and relieve the drain plug so old grease can leave. Record every greasing event so a temperature change can be correlated with maintenance history.
