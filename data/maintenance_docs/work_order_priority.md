# Work Order Prioritisation and Escalation
## Priority levels
Emergency: safety risk or imminent failure, act immediately. High: inspect within the current shift. Medium: schedule within 24 hours. Low: plan at the next routine stop.
## Escalation
Escalate to the maintenance supervisor when risk is high, evidence is conflicting, or required spares are unavailable. Include the evidence, the confidence level and the alternatives considered.
## Closing the loop
Close each work order with findings, parts used and measurements after repair, so predictions can be compared with outcomes.
