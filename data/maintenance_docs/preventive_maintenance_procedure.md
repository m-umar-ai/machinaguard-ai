# Preventive Maintenance Procedure
## Scheduling a planned inspection
When condition monitoring shows a developing fault, schedule a planned inspection within the next shift change rather than waiting for run-to-failure. Prepare spare parts, a technician checklist and a rollback plan before stopping the line.
## Post-maintenance verification
After repair, run the machine unloaded and then at nominal load. Confirm vibration, temperature and energy return to baseline. Record the new baseline values in the maintenance log.
## Sensor calibration
Calibrate vibration and temperature sensors at regular intervals. Replace sensors whose readings disagree with redundant instruments.
