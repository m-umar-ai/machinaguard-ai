# Condition-Based Maintenance Strategy
## Why condition-based
Run-to-failure causes unplanned downtime and secondary damage; fixed-interval replacement wastes good parts. Condition-based maintenance acts when measured condition shows deterioration, balancing cost and risk.
## Elements
A programme needs reliable sensing, a healthy baseline, alarm rules, a diagnosis step, a decision with human authorisation, and feedback from inspection findings to improve the rules.
## Human in the loop
Automated analytics should recommend, not execute. A qualified person approves shutdowns and part replacement, and inspection results are fed back to the model.
