# Compressors and Compressed Air Systems
## Common faults
Compressors fail through valve wear, lubrication loss, overheating and clogged filters. Rising discharge temperature with falling pressure indicates internal leakage or cooling problems.
## Monitoring
Track discharge temperature, pressure, motor current and vibration. Leaks waste substantial energy, so rising energy per delivered volume is an early sign.
## Maintenance
Replace filters and separators on schedule, drain condensate, check oil level and inspect belts and couplings.
